Everything your parking team does, in one place

For site admins in the web console, and attendants at the gate on any phone.

  • Live floor plan

    Every slot on every level, coloured by status: vacant, occupied, reserved or under maintenance. The view refreshes on its own.

  • Gate entry by plate

    Attendants scan or type a number plate on a phone to record entries and exits, and see which slots are free.

  • Employee passes

    Your site admin adds employees and their vehicles, one at a time or from a CSV, and approves or rejects each pass request.

  • Reports and exports

    Utilisation, occupancy trend and vehicle mix for your site. Export the entry and exit log to CSV.

  • Many sites, one console

    Run every site you manage from one sign-in, and switch between them from the header.

  • Valet desk (optional)

    Check cars in, track each car's status, and log retrieval requests at the valet desk.
