Privacy note

This note covers parkflows.in, the ParkFlows website, and the details you send us through the Book a demo form.

What we collect

Only what you type into the form: your name, company, work email, phone number, city, facility type, the approximate number of parking slots and sites, how you'd like us to contact you, your message, and the time you agreed to be contacted. We don't store your IP address or browser details with your request, or anywhere else in the ParkFlows system. Like most websites, our hosting provider, Google Cloud, keeps standard server logs that include the network address of each visit, for up to 30 days, to keep the service running and secure. We only look at them to fix problems or stop abuse.

Why we use it

To contact you about a ParkFlows demo, the way you asked us to. We don't sell it or share it for marketing.

Who can see it

Only the ParkFlows team members who handle demo requests. Your request is kept in the ParkFlows system, not in a mailing list.

How long we keep it

Up to 12 months after you send it. After that it is deleted.

Asking us to delete it sooner

Write to sales@parkflows.in from the email address you used, and we'll delete your request.

This note covers parkflows.in, the ParkFlows website, and what happens when you email or call us about a demo.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us: the contents of your email, such as your name, company, contact details and anything you tell us about your site, or the details you give us on a phone call. Like any email, your message also carries standard email information, such as your email address and when it was sent. This website itself doesn't collect any personal information.

Why we use it

To reply to you and arrange a ParkFlows demo, the way you asked us to. We don't sell it or share it for marketing.

Who can see it

Only the ParkFlows team members who handle demo enquiries. Emails are kept in our business mailbox, which our email provider runs for us. We don't add you to a mailing list.

How long we keep it

Up to 12 months after our last contact with you. After that we delete it.

Asking us to delete it sooner

Write to sales@parkflows.in from the email address you used, and we'll delete your enquiry.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts.
